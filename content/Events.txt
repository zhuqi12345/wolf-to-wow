--Spring Dog Run
As the snow melts and the city starts to shake off its winter coat, there's no better way to celebrate the season than with your four-legged best friend by your side. The From Wolf to Wow Spring Dog Run is our way of welcoming the warmer months with wagging tails and muddy paws. Whether you're a seasoned runner or more of a casual stroller, this event is all about getting outside, breathing in that fresh spring air, and letting your pup do what they do best — run, sniff, and be absolutely ridiculous in public.
The event features a fun and relaxed 2 km route through a local park, designed to be accessible for dogs and owners of all fitness levels. The course winds through open green space with plenty of room for your dog to enjoy the sights and smells of the season. Water stations for both humans and dogs are set up along the route, and our staff will be on hand throughout to make sure everyone — two-legged and four-legged alike — has a safe and enjoyable run.
After the run, stick around for a casual cool-down in the park where you can meet fellow dog owners, swap stories, and let the pups socialize to their hearts' content. From Wolf to Wow will have treats on hand for the dogs (and maybe a coffee or two for the humans). It's the perfect way to kick off the spring season and a great reminder that the best running partner you'll ever have has four paws and an unbeatable attitude.

--Dog Days of Summer Beach Meet
When the summer heat is at its peak, there's only one place to be — and your dog already knows it. The From Wolf to Wow Dog Days of Summer Beach Meet is our favourite event of the year, bringing together dogs and their humans for a splashing, bounding, gloriously chaotic afternoon at the water's edge. Whether your pup is a fearless swimmer who hits the waves at full speed or a cautious wader who just wants to get their paws wet, this event has something for every kind of water dog.
The beach meet is an informal, drop-in style gathering where you can show up whenever works for you and stay as long as your dog's energy allows — which, if our experience is anything to go by, is a very long time. There's plenty of open space for fetch, frisbee, and full-tilt zoomies along the shoreline. We'll have a shaded rest area set up with fresh water and snacks for the dogs, because all that swimming works up quite an appetite.
It wouldn't be a From Wolf to Wow event without a little friendly competition, so keep an eye out for our unofficial "best splash" and "most dramatic fetch" awards — voted on by attendees and taken extremely seriously by absolutely nobody. It's a laid-back, sun-soaked afternoon that perfectly captures what summer with a dog is all about. Come ready to get wet, sandy, and completely charmed by the hundreds of happy dogs who will absolutely make your day.

--End of Summer BBQ
As the long days of summer start to wind down and the first hints of autumn creep into the evening air, From Wolf to Wow hosts its most beloved community gathering of the year — the End of Summer BBQ. This is our chance to bring together the incredible community of dog owners, dog lovers, and dog enthusiasts who make our little corner of the city such a special place. Think sizzling burgers, good music, happy dogs, and the kind of easy conversation that only happens when everyone at the party has brought their best friend along.
The BBQ takes place in a local park with plenty of open space for the dogs to mingle while their humans do the same. We fire up the grill for a full afternoon of food and fun, with vegetarian options available alongside the classic BBQ fare. Dogs are of course fully catered to with their own special menu of dog-safe treats prepared by our team, because no pup should have to sit and watch their human eat without getting something delicious of their own.
The End of Summer BBQ is also our opportunity to look back on a great season of events, thank our amazing community for their support, and give a few well-deserved shoutouts to some of our most dedicated daycare and grooming regulars. It's a relaxed, joyful afternoon with no agenda other than good food, great company, and the simple pleasure of watching a bunch of happy dogs have the time of their lives. Come hungry, come happy, and come ready to say a fond farewell to another wonderful summer.

--Fall Park Leaf Romp
There is nothing — absolutely nothing — quite like watching a dog discover a giant pile of freshly raked autumn leaves for the first time. The From Wolf to Wow Fall Park Leaf Romp celebrates one of the great joys of the season, bringing dogs and their humans together for an afternoon of colour, crunch, and pure unbridled chaos in the park. As the trees put on their most spectacular show of the year, we invite you to slow down, soak it all in, and let your pup remind you how to find pure joy in the simplest things.
The Leaf Romp takes place at a local park at the peak of fall colour, when the grounds are blanketed in red, orange, and gold. We'll have designated leaf pile zones where dogs can dive, dig, and generally demolish hours of careful raking in approximately four seconds flat. There's a marked walking trail through the most scenic part of the park for those who want to take in the fall foliage at a more leisurely pace, and plenty of open space for off-leash play in designated areas.
Warm drinks will be available for the humans — because a crisp fall afternoon in the park basically demands a hot chocolate — and our team will be on hand with dog treats and plenty of enthusiasm. We'll also be running our popular fall photo station, where you can get a gorgeous autumn snapshot of your pup surrounded by leaves to use as your new phone wallpaper, profile picture, or holiday card. The Fall Park Leaf Romp is one of those events that never gets old, no matter how many years you've been coming — mostly because the dogs make sure it's different every single time.

--Doggy Pictures with Santa
The holiday season wouldn't be complete without a visit from the big man himself — and this year, he's bringing dog treats. The From Wolf to Wow Doggy Pictures with Santa event is one of our most popular and most heartwarming traditions, giving your pup the chance to sit with Santa Claus for a professional holiday photo you'll treasure for years to come. Whether your dog is a natural in front of the camera or more of a "creative challenge," our patient and experienced photographer will make sure you walk away with a shot worth framing.
The event runs over a single festive weekend, with time slots available to book in advance so you're not waiting around with an increasingly impatient dog on a busy holiday Saturday. Our studio space will be decked out in full Christmas regalia — think twinkling lights, festive garlands, and a very convincing Santa throne — creating the perfect backdrop for your holiday card photo. Santa himself is fully briefed on dog etiquette and has been thoroughly vetted for treat-dispensing capability.
All proceeds from booking fees go directly to a local animal shelter, making this event as meaningful as it is adorable. We'll also have a donation station on site where you can drop off items from the shelter's wish list — food, blankets, toys, and supplies are always gratefully received. Booking fills up fast every year, so keep an eye on our social media and website for the date announcement and grab your slot early. Because nothing says happy holidays quite like a photo of your dog wearing reindeer antlers and looking mildly confused next to Santa Claus.
