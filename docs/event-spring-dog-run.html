Spring Dog Run

Ask About This Event All Events

As the snow melts and the city starts to shake off its winter coat, there's no better way to celebrate the season than with your four-legged best friend by your side. The From Wolf to Wow Spring Dog Run is our way of welcoming the warmer months with wagging tails and muddy paws. Whether you're a seasoned runner or more of a casual stroller, this event is all about getting outside, breathing in that fresh spring air, and letting your pup do what they do best — run, sniff, and be absolutely ridiculous in public. The event features a fun and relaxed 2 km route through a local park, designed to be accessible for dogs and owners of all fitness levels. The course winds through open green space with plenty of room for your dog to enjoy the sights and smells of the season. Water stations for both humans and dogs are set up along the route, and our staff will be on hand throughout to make sure everyone — two-legged and four-legged alike — has a safe and enjoyable run. After the run, stick around for a casual cool-down in the park where you can meet fellow dog owners, swap stories, and let the pups socialize to their hearts' content. From Wolf to Wow will have treats on hand for the dogs (and maybe a coffee or two for the humans). It's the perfect way to kick off the spring season and a great reminder that the best running partner you'll ever have has four paws and an unbeatable attitude.
