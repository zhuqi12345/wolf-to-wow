About From Wolf to Wow

New owners, established trust, better booking.

From Wolf to Wow is a dog grooming and daycare business in Edmonton. The shop has been part of the neighbourhood for years, and the new owners are focused on making the customer experience as dependable as the in-person care.

That means clear services, visible availability, practical estimates, and a simple account area where customers can keep dog details current and cancel appointments when plans change.

How we work

Small enough to know each dog.

Daycare stays small enough for staff supervision, and grooming appointments are scheduled by groomer and start time so every dog gets focused handling.

Care Notes

Customers can save dog names, sizes, and notes so booking forms are faster and less error-prone.

Clear Follow-Up

Bookings are recorded immediately and marked pending until staff confirm the appointment.

Useful Estimates

Booking estimates update from dog size, selected services, and groomer so customers can check the range before requesting an appointment.
