Doggy Pictures with Santa

Ask About This Event All Events

The holiday season wouldn't be complete without a visit from the big man himself — and this year, he's bringing dog treats. The From Wolf to Wow Doggy Pictures with Santa event is one of our most popular and most heartwarming traditions, giving your pup the chance to sit with Santa Claus for a professional holiday photo you'll treasure for years to come. Whether your dog is a natural in front of the camera or more of a "creative challenge," our patient and experienced photographer will make sure you walk away with a shot worth framing. The event runs over a single festive weekend, with time slots available to book in advance so you're not waiting around with an increasingly impatient dog on a busy holiday Saturday. Our studio space will be decked out in full Christmas regalia — think twinkling lights, festive garlands, and a very convincing Santa throne — creating the perfect backdrop for your holiday card photo. Santa himself is fully briefed on dog etiquette and has been thoroughly vetted for treat-dispensing capability. All proceeds from booking fees go directly to a local animal shelter, making this event as meaningful as it is adorable. We'll also have a donation station on site where you can drop off items from the shelter's wish list — food, blankets, toys, and supplies are always gratefully received. Booking fills up fast every year, so keep an eye on our social media and website for the date announcement and grab your slot early. Because nothing says happy holidays quite like a photo of your dog wearing reindeer antlers and looking mildly confused next to Santa Claus.
