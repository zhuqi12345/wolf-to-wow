Meet your groomers

Choose the handling style that fits your dog.

Each groomer can perform every listed service, but every dog has a different pace. These profiles help customers choose a person before they use the booking grid, where each groomer is limited to one dog per time slot.

Sarah Mitchell

Calm handling and coat maintenance

Sarah is a steady, detail-focused groomer who works well with dogs that need a quieter pace and a predictable routine.

Best fit for first visits, small dogs, and regular bath-and-brush care.

Jake Thornton

Everyday grooms and tidy coat resets

Jake keeps appointments efficient without rushing the dog, with a practical style suited to active family pets.

Best fit for medium coats, maintenance trims, and full groom appointments.

Priya Patel

Face, feet, and finishing details

Priya has a gentle, precise approach for dogs that need careful trimming around sensitive areas.

Best fit for detail work, tidy faces, nail care, and dogs that need extra patience.

Cody Brennan

Bath visits and upbeat handling

Cody brings an easygoing pace to bath appointments and helps energetic dogs settle into the grooming routine.

Best fit for bath visits, younger dogs, and dogs that do better with a relaxed tone.

Linda Kowalski

Senior dogs and comfort-first care

Linda focuses on steady handling and comfort checks, especially when a dog needs a slower appointment.

Best fit for senior dogs, sensitive dogs, and longer coat maintenance sessions.
