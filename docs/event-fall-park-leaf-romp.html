Fall Park Leaf Romp

Ask About This Event All Events

There is nothing — absolutely nothing — quite like watching a dog discover a giant pile of freshly raked autumn leaves for the first time. The From Wolf to Wow Fall Park Leaf Romp celebrates one of the great joys of the season, bringing dogs and their humans together for an afternoon of colour, crunch, and pure unbridled chaos in the park. As the trees put on their most spectacular show of the year, we invite you to slow down, soak it all in, and let your pup remind you how to find pure joy in the simplest things. The Leaf Romp takes place at a local park at the peak of fall colour, when the grounds are blanketed in red, orange, and gold. We'll have designated leaf pile zones where dogs can dive, dig, and generally demolish hours of careful raking in approximately four seconds flat. There's a marked walking trail through the most scenic part of the park for those who want to take in the fall foliage at a more leisurely pace, and plenty of open space for off-leash play in designated areas. Warm drinks will be available for the humans — because a crisp fall afternoon in the park basically demands a hot chocolate — and our team will be on hand with dog treats and plenty of enthusiasm. We'll also be running our popular fall photo station, where you can get a gorgeous autumn snapshot of your pup surrounded by leaves to use as your new phone wallpaper, profile picture, or holiday card. The Fall Park Leaf Romp is one of those events that never gets old, no matter how many years you've been coming — mostly because the dogs make sure it's different every single time.
