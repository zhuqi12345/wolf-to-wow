What dogs get

Routine, supervision, and room to settle.

Dogs are grouped with staff supervision, structured rest, potty breaks, and handling notes. Customers can keep notes with each dog profile so the team sees important details during booking.

Required health and behaviour information can be discussed before the first daycare day. From Wolf to Wow reserves the right to refuse service if a dog appears sick, injured, aggressive, or unsafe for the group.
