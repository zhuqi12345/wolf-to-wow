Dog Days of Summer Beach Meet

Ask About This Event All Events

When the summer heat is at its peak, there's only one place to be — and your dog already knows it. The From Wolf to Wow Dog Days of Summer Beach Meet is our favourite event of the year, bringing together dogs and their humans for a splashing, bounding, gloriously chaotic afternoon at the water's edge. Whether your pup is a fearless swimmer who hits the waves at full speed or a cautious wader who just wants to get their paws wet, this event has something for every kind of water dog. The beach meet is an informal, drop-in style gathering where you can show up whenever works for you and stay as long as your dog's energy allows — which, if our experience is anything to go by, is a very long time. There's plenty of open space for fetch, frisbee, and full-tilt zoomies along the shoreline. We'll have a shaded rest area set up with fresh water and snacks for the dogs, because all that swimming works up quite an appetite. It wouldn't be a From Wolf to Wow event without a little friendly competition, so keep an eye out for our unofficial "best splash" and "most dramatic fetch" awards — voted on by attendees and taken extremely seriously by absolutely nobody. It's a laid-back, sun-soaked afternoon that perfectly captures what summer with a dog is all about. Come ready to get wet, sandy, and completely charmed by the hundreds of happy dogs who will absolutely make your day.
